# Language

English (`en`, LTR) and Hebrew (`he`, RTL) are equally supported.
The first visit uses Hebrew when the browser's primary language is Hebrew; otherwise English.
A manual language choice is stored locally and takes precedence on later visits.

regular updates, handover, and technical partner. Avoid developer jargon and revenue promises.
