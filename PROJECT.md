# Joseph Tech Solutions

A bilingual business website for Joseph Sabag, aimed at small businesses, startup founders,
and people looking for a technical partner for an idea.

Three offers: launch an idea, rescue an unfinished product, and automate business operations.
Work can use a project contract, a selective equity partnership, or a mutually agreed mix.
The website does not promise revenue or specific business results.

The design follows `03-sheets-v6.html`: white sheets in a dark frame, blue rounded contact
buttons, a pixel animation, eight problem scenarios, an iceberg, and a sunset ending.
English and Hebrew have complete authored copy. The new profile portrait is used throughout.

All primary contact links open WhatsApp at +972546187549 with a localized draft message.
No message is sent automatically. The site has no forms, accounts, or business API.

Canonical domain: https://joseph-tech-solutions.dev/.
The previous versioned clients, backend, chat, blog, product pages, and legal pages are retired.
Their history remains in Git; their former routes now return 404.
