# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, technical leads, and founders who need to quickly understand Joseph Sabag's fit, technical range, work velocity, and unusual self-made path from business automation into production AI and full-stack engineering.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio should work as a sharp proof-of-work journey, not a static resume. It should make the visitor understand what is different about Joseph: he spots operational friction, builds practical automation, ships production systems, and keeps compressing the path from idea to useful product.

Success means a recruiter can answer three questions in under a minute: what kind of engineer he is, why his path is unusual, and which projects prove it.

## Brand Personality

Precise, relentless, and practical. The voice should feel direct, technical, and alive, with confidence coming from shipped evidence instead of self-promotion.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, oversized hero metrics, repeated identical card grids, decorative tech-bro neon, vague AI buzzwords, resume walls, and portfolio pages that make every project look equally important.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the journey: show the progression from spotting manual work to shipping reliable AI/full-stack products.
2. Make evidence scannable: every claim should connect to a project, production result, or measurable outcome.
3. Prioritize recruiter speed: the first screen should answer identity, edge, credibility, and contact.
4. Preserve technical sharpness: use dense, clean structure without hiding behind decorative effects.
5. Show range through contrast: production jobs, mobile apps, extensions, automation tools, and open-source systems should feel like one coherent operating pattern.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable links and controls, reduced-motion support, readable line lengths, and responsive layouts that keep recruiter-critical content visible on mobile.
